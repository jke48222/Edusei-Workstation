I rebuilt my portfolio from scratch, and it is live: jalenedusei.com

I wanted it to feel less like a PDF and more like watching the work happen. So the home page is a single video that scrubs frame by frame as you scroll, with my projects, skills, and experience layered over it. The whole thing is built with no animation library, just requestAnimationFrame and an IntersectionObserver doing the work.

And because one portfolio was not enough, there is a second one hiding inside it: a 3D workstation you can actually explore, with a retro CRT terminal and clickable hardware, built in React Three Fiber and holding 60fps even on a phone.

A few things under the hood:
• Scroll-scrubbed video hero (rAF eases the clip to your scroll position)
• A live, filterable project archive with real in-page previews
• My full toolset, from STM32 and Verilog to React, Supabase, and the Claude API
• React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind, Zustand, and React Three Fiber
• The hero video was generated with Higgsfield
