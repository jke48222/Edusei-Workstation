# Promo / showcase video prompt

A prompt for generating a short (15 to 25 second) promotional video for the portfolio
at jalenedusei.com. Written for an AI text-to-video tool (Higgsfield, Runway, Sora, Kling).
Use the one-paragraph version as the main prompt; use the shot list to guide edits or
multi-shot generation.

---

## One-paragraph prompt

A sleek, premium product-reveal film for a software engineer's portfolio website. Monochrome bone-white and ink-black color palette, clean DM Sans typography, calm and confident pacing. Open on a tight macro shot of a single eye reflecting scrolling code, then pull back to reveal a photoreal 3D portrait that dissolves into a glowing wireframe mesh as the camera tracks down. Crisp white-on-dark and dark-on-light text elements slide and fade into frame in layers, never harsh, always smooth. Cut to a grid of glassmorphic project cards floating over the moving footage, each lighting up on hover. Transition through a swirling fiber-optic light burst into a retro CRT terminal glowing on a 3D desk, green phosphor text typing itself out, then orbit the desk to reveal clickable hardware. End on the wordmark "Jalen Edusei" in bold lowercase, with the line "Building software with real impact" and the URL jalenedusei.com. Cinematic, high contrast, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, no people speaking, ambient electronic underscore.

## Shot list

1. **0:00–0:03 — Macro open.** Extreme close-up of an eye, faint code reflected in it. Slow push-in. Bone background.
2. **0:03–0:06 — The portrait.** Pull back to a photoreal 3D head; as it rotates, the surface becomes a glowing white wireframe mesh. Ink headline "Building software with real impact." fades in lower-left.
3. **0:06–0:09 — Reveal layers.** Side text and small project labels slide in additively from the right, stacking, narrow columns, monospaced captions.
4. **0:09–0:13 — Work grid.** Cut to a 2-to-3 column grid of clean white project cards floating over dark moving footage; one card plays a UI clip, another shows a live website, a third a circuit board. Cards lift and glow on hover.
5. **0:13–0:16 — Skills sweep.** Quick montage of monochrome technology logos snapping into a grid (C++, Unity, React, STM32, Figma, Claude).
6. **0:16–0:20 — Into the machine.** Camera dives through a swirling warm fiber-optic light burst and lands in front of a retro CRT monitor on a wooden 3D desk; green phosphor terminal text types "EDUSEI WORKSTATION v2.026". Slow orbit reveals clickable hardware on the desk.
7. **0:20–0:24 — Sign-off.** Snap to bone background. Oversized ink wordmark "Jalen Edusei," tagline "Computer Systems Engineer · UGA '26," and the URL "jalenedusei.com." Cursor blinks. Cut to black.

## Style keywords

`premium portfolio reveal, monochrome bone and ink, DM Sans, glassmorphism, wireframe head, CRT terminal, fiber-optic light burst, shallow depth of field, high contrast, subtle film grain, smooth eases, ambient electronic score, no dialogue, 24fps cinematic`

## Negative prompt

`cluttered, neon rainbow colors, busy stock-motion graphics, cheesy transitions, watermarks, distorted text, low resolution, talking head, fast jittery cuts`
